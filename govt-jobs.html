Frequently asked questions

Which jobs are listed on this page?

Only government civil engineering recruitment — central and state notifications such as SSC JE, RRB/RRC Junior Engineer, State PSC AE/JE, PWD, CPWD, NHAI, MES, metro rail, irrigation and water resources departments, municipal bodies and civil vacancies in PSUs. Private-sector jobs are on the private jobs page.

How often is this table updated?

New notifications are pulled automatically from official government sources (NCS portal, Employment News and state notifications) and published after a review step so wrong or duplicate entries never reach the table. The “Updated on” figure shows the latest refresh.

What is the minimum qualification for these posts?

It varies by post. Most Junior Engineer posts need a Diploma in Civil Engineering, Assistant/Executive Engineer posts need B.E./B.Tech Civil, and some helper/draftsman or survey posts accept 10th/12th with an ITI or drafting certificate. The qualification column shows what each notification asks for.

What does “days left” mean?

It is the number of days remaining until the last date of online application, computed from the deadline in the official notice. Rows marked Expired have crossed their closing date — keep “Hide expired” ticked to see only live vacancies.

Where do I actually apply?

Always on the official website linked in each row. CivilCareer only summarises the notification; we never collect applications and never charge a fee. Verify every detail on the official notification before applying.

Can I get an alert when a new notification appears?

Yes — set up free job alerts on the job alerts page, and follow the Telegram channel for instant government vacancy posts.

Data is compiled from official recruitment portals and notifications. CivilCareer is not a government entity. If a deadline looks outdated, trust the official notification, not this table.
