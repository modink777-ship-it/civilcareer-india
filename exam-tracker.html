About the Exam Tracker

The CivilCareer Exam Tracker follows every major civil engineering recruitment examination in India — GATE Civil Engineering, SSC Junior Engineer, RRB Junior Engineer, UPSC Engineering Services (ESE), State Public Service Commission Assistant Engineer exams (KPSC, TSPSC, MPSC, UPPSC) and PSU recruitment (NHPC, NTPC and more). For each exam you can see the notification date, application start and end dates, exam schedule, result date, expected vacancies and eligibility summary, with a direct link to the official website. Star the exams you are preparing for to keep them at the top of this page — no login or account is needed. Tap Alert Me and we will notify you on WhatsApp when an exam's status changes: notification out, applications open, admit card, or result. CivilCareer is an independent information platform and never charges candidates — always verify every detail in the original official notification before applying.
