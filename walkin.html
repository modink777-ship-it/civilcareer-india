Walk-In Interviews This Week — Civil Engineering

Direct hiring drives across India — no application needed, just show up with your resume. Dates, venues, timings, documents and contacts, all in one place.

⚡ Walk-ins move fast. Reach the venue early, carry every document listed, and never pay anyone for attending an interview — genuine walk-in drives are always free. Verify the company before you go.

How to prepare for a walk-in interview

1. Carry everything

Multiple printed resumes, all marksheets, ID proof, passport photos and your certificates — as listed on each drive. Originals plus photocopies.

2. Arrive early

Walk-in drives often stop taking candidates by early afternoon. Reaching the venue within the first two hours gives you the best chance.

3. Know the company

Spend ten minutes on the company's projects before you go. Interviewers notice candidates who already understand what the firm builds.

4. Stay safe

Genuine walk-in drives never charge fees. If anyone demands money for "registration" or "confirmation", walk away and report it.
