## Reading the table

- **Today we lose to NCS (26)** — the only serious score near ours — on verification, alerts and government depth. Those are exactly the P0+P1 items.
- **Civil-only relevance (5) and zero ad clutter (5) are structural wins** no competitor can copy without abandoning their business model. Naukri/Indeed/foundit monetise by diluting; Sarkari Result/FreeJobAlert bury users under ads and cover every branch of engineering.
- **After P0+P1 we lead on the combination** that matters to the user: the only civil-only, ad-free, verified, deep-government platform with modern alerts. Generalists keep bigger totals of listings; none of them wins on the axes a civil engineer actually chooses on.
- **Exam prep stays a 2 after P1** deliberately — the study hub is P2. Do not claim 5 before it exists.
- **Scam protection becomes a 4** through: mandatory expiry, scam-language auto-detection on submissions, fee-request flags, Report flow with a review-time target, and the public Known Scams page (P2 item pulls it to 5).
