Civil Engineer Salary in India 2026

The average salary for a civil engineer in India ranges from ₹3.2 lakh to ₹14 lakh per year depending on role, city and experience. Entry-level site engineers earn around ₹3.2 lakh per year, while experienced quantity surveyors and planning engineers at MNCs earn up to ₹14 lakh or more. Government junior engineers start near ₹4.2 lakh under the 7th Pay Commission.

About this salary data

CivilCareer's salary pages collect anonymous annual compensation reports from civil engineers working across India — on sites, in planning departments, in consultancies and in government offices. Every figure shown is an aggregate: we display ranges and medians, never individual submissions. Government and PSU figures reflect Pay-Commission and PSU scales; private and MNC figures come from engineers like you. A submission marked verified has been reviewed by the CivilCareer team against job postings and market ranges; unverified figures still count toward ranges until review completes. Salary negotiation starts with knowing the numbers — and no one should have to ask a recruiter twice.
