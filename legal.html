Terms · Privacy · Disclaimer

Trust starts with clear responsibilities.

Important: This is an early-stage practical policy, not legal advice. Obtain qualified legal review before enabling payments, advertising, accounts or commercial-scale data processing.

Independent information platform

CivilCareer organizes summaries and links to original employment, government recruitment, examination and study-resource sources. CivilCareer is not an employer, recruitment agency, government organization, examination authority or representative of any listed organization.

No guarantee

CivilCareer does not guarantee accuracy, availability, employment, selection, salary, interview, appointment, examination results, visa or any outcome. Dates and requirements may change. The original official notification or employer page is authoritative.

Job and examination safety

  • Never pay for an interview, appointment, selection, offer letter, recruitment or access to a job.
  • Never share OTP, CVV, passwords, UPI PIN, banking credentials or unnecessary identity documents.
  • Verify the employer, department, notification number, eligibility, dates, fees and application process independently.
  • Report suspected cybercrime through cybercrime.gov.in.

Privacy

Public browsing does not require an account. CivilCareer does not request Aadhaar, PAN, bank credentials or application documents. Privacy-conscious analytics use a random browser identifier, page path, broad device type, referring domain and country code. Raw IP addresses are not intentionally stored by CivilCareer. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process limited technical logs for security and service operation.

Submissions and reports

Employer jobs, resources and reports are stored privately for administrator review. Submission does not guarantee publication. Do not submit sensitive personal data, private group messages, unlawful material or deceptive claims.

Study materials and copyright

Submit only materials you created, own or have permission to distribute. Rights holders may request correction or removal through the Report a Problem page.

Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Advertising and affiliate links are not enabled at launch. If added later, they must be clearly identified and must never resemble an official government notification or application button.

Corrections and removal

CivilCareer may correct, unpublish, archive or remove expired, disputed, suspicious or unsupported content. Contact modin7174@gmail.com or use the private reporting form.

Founder accountability

The Founder / Product Owner is the single accountable owner for product direction, content standards, source verification, safety and final publishing decisions. User safety and accuracy take priority over growth and revenue.
