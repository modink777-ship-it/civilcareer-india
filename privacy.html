Privacy Policy

How CivilCareer handles personal data

This policy covers the India-only CivilCareer platform. It explains what we collect, why we use it, and how you can request correction or deletion.

What we collect

  • Account identity and authentication data handled by our authentication provider.
  • Profile details, saved jobs, application-tracker entries and alert preferences you choose to enter.
  • Submission data for jobs, resources, reports and contact requests.
  • Privacy-conscious analytics such as a random browser identifier, page path, broad device type, referring domain and country code.

What we do not request

Do not submit Aadhaar, PAN, UPI PIN, bank passwords, OTPs or other unnecessary financial or authentication secrets.

Why we process data

We use personal data to provide career tools, save preferences, support candidate features, moderate submitted content, prevent abuse, measure usage and respond to grievances.

Retention and deletion

We retain candidate data only while needed for the relevant account, feature, safety or legal purpose. Use the account controls or contact route to request deletion. Aggregated non-identifying statistics may be retained.

Third-party infrastructure

Hosted infrastructure and authentication/database providers support the platform. Technical providers may process limited service logs. Server-side API keys are not exposed to the browser.

Your choices

You may access or correct account information, remove saved items, and request deletion of personal data. For a grievance or correction, use the Contact page.

India DPDP Act, 2023

This is a practical product policy and should receive professional legal review before commercial-scale processing. Notices and processes may be updated as applicable law changes.
