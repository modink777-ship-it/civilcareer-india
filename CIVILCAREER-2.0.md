# CivilCareer 2.0

CivilCareer is repositioned as an India-wide, civil-engineering-only career platform.

## Public structure
- All Civil Jobs
- Government Civil Jobs
- MNC Civil Jobs
- PSU Civil Jobs
- Fresher Civil Jobs
- Civil Engineering Internships
- For You
- Civil & Government Exams
- Free Study Materials

## Classification
MNC remains a private employer subtype (`company_type=MNC`) so the same job is not duplicated in storage. PSU remains a public-sector classification.

## Scope
Government coverage is national and civil-related only. Private/MNC coverage is civil-engineering specific: construction, EPC, infrastructure, structural, BIM, quantity surveying, planning, QA/QC, contracts, surveying, water, transportation, geotechnical and related roles.

## Safety
Discovery remains review-first. Public sources and permitted feeds are collected into drafts; nothing is auto-published. LinkedIn and Naukri are not scraped without authorization.
