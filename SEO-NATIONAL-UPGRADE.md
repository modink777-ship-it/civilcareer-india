# CivilCareer SEO & Discoverability Upgrade

This free, no-database-migration upgrade improves organic discoverability and sharing for the national portal.

## Included
- Dynamic title, description and canonical URL for individual jobs, exams and study materials.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata for detail pages.
- Schema.org JobPosting structured data for job detail pages.
- Schema.org Article structured data for exam and study-material detail pages.
- BreadcrumbList structured data for detail pages.
- Crawlable internal links from job, exam and material cards to their dedicated URLs.
- Existing SPA navigation and functionality are preserved.

## Deployment
Replace the project files in GitHub as usual. No Supabase migration or paid service is required.
