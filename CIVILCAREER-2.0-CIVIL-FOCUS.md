# CivilCareer 2.0 — Civil Focus Upgrade

## Product direction
CivilCareer is now positioned as an India-wide Civil Engineering career platform. Government content is civil-related only, while private, MNC, PSU, fresher and internship paths remain civil-engineering focused.

## Public exam filtering
The public Exams page now filters out general government exams unless the record contains a civil/engineering-related signal. Exam filters are focused on AE Civil, JE Civil, GATE Civil, SSC JE Civil and RRB JE Civil.

## Study materials filtering
The public Study Materials page now excludes general competitive-exam resources and shows only civil-engineering technical resources such as Structural, Geotechnical, Transportation, RCC, Surveying, Estimation and BIM.

## Visual refresh
Added a Civil Engineering visual treatment to the homepage using construction/engineering photography as background and feature panels. The layout remains responsive and uses external image URLs so no image-hosting service or paid asset subscription is required.

## Government jobs
Government listings are filtered through the same civil-engineering relevance rules before display. Karnataka remains available as a state/location, but is no longer the site's product identity.

## Important
Existing database records are not deleted by this UI-only focus change. Non-civil exam/material records can remain in the database for admin/history, but are not displayed in the public civil-focused sections.
