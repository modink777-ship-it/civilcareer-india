Roles in demand

Civil engineering hiring in India concentrates around a known set of roles. Each one below links straight to current openings: Site Engineer, Planning Engineer, Structural Engineer, Quantity Surveyor, Billing Engineer, QA/QC Engineer, Estimation Engineer, Highway Engineer, Transportation Engineer, Geotechnical Engineer, BIM Engineer, Project Engineer, Contracts Engineer and Safety Engineer.

Where these roles sit

Construction and infrastructure work — roads, metro, railways, bridges, airports, water and industrial projects — hires mainly for site and planning roles. Consultancies, PMCs and EPC contractors also hire designers and quantity surveyors. Government departments and PSUs recruit through their own examinations and notifications on the Government Civil Jobs and Exams pages.
