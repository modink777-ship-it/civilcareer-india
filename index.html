AI-assisted job importer

Import from a link or pasted vacancy text

Use either method, or use both for the best result. For LinkedIn, paste the link and copy the complete vacancy text because LinkedIn often hides details from automated readers.

AI never publishes automatically. Verify the title, company, location, dates, eligibility and original source in the editor before saving. If only a LinkedIn URL is supplied, extraction may remain limited by LinkedIn.

Notification reader

Import an official exam PDF

Select a text-based government notification PDF. Gemini organizes the notification into a complete recruitment listing with overview, posts, vacancies, qualification, dates, age limits, fees, selection, syllabus, application steps, official links and FAQs. You review everything before publishing.

Scanned-image PDFs require OCR and may not work in this version. Nothing is published until you review and save it.

Monetization is not enabled

No fake revenue is displayed. Featured jobs, configurable pricing, payments and advertising can be introduced only after trust, traffic and legal foundations are ready.
