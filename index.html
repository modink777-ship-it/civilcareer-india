Roles in demand

Civil engineering hiring in India concentrates around a known set of roles. Each one below links straight to current openings: Site Engineer, Planning Engineer, Structural Engineer, Quantity Surveyor, Billing Engineer, QA/QC Engineer, Estimation Engineer, Highway Engineer, Transportation Engineer, Geotechnical Engineer, BIM Engineer, Project Engineer, Contracts Engineer and Safety Engineer.

Where these roles sit

Construction and infrastructure work — roads, metro, railways, bridges, airports, water and industrial projects — hires mainly for site and planning roles. Consultancies, PMCs and EPC contractors also hire designers and quantity surveyors. Government departments and PSUs recruit through their own examinations and notifications on the Government Civil Jobs and Exams pages.

Rather than picking a path in the abstract, set your role, project type, skills and career stage in For You. CivilCareer will then show which live jobs fit you and which skills are still missing, and link the free Study Materials that close those gaps.

AI-assisted job importer

Import from a link or pasted vacancy text

Use either method, or use both for the best result. For LinkedIn, paste the link and copy the complete vacancy text because LinkedIn often hides details from automated readers.

AI never publishes automatically. Verify the title, company, location, dates, eligibility and original source in the editor before saving. If only a LinkedIn URL is supplied, extraction may remain limited by LinkedIn.

Automatic civil-job discovery

Civil vacancies for India, discovered every 4 hours

Every 4 hours the discovery model searches job platforms, India job feeds and vacancy news, and reads the career pages of 35 top Indian construction, infrastructure and real-estate companies. Every lead is India-only and civil-only, so nothing irrelevant reaches the queue. Nothing is published automatically.
