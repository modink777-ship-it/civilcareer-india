Automatic civil-job discovery

Civil vacancies for India, discovered every 4 hours

Every 4 hours the discovery model searches job platforms, India job feeds and vacancy news, and reads the career pages of 35 top Indian construction, infrastructure and real-estate companies. Every lead is India-only and civil-only, so nothing irrelevant reaches the queue. New matches are saved as unpublished drafts for your review. Nothing is published automatically.

🔑 Connect a job source to get results

The free public feeds (Remotive, CareerJet, Findwork) are included but rarely have India civil engineering jobs. For real India-specific results, add at least one API key in Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables.
