📥 How to collect jobs

  1. Paste the ad text you already have — a WhatsApp message, an email forward, a printed notice.
  2. Upload a screenshot or PDF (job posters, recruitment notices). OCR reads it when a vision key is configured; otherwise the item waits for you to paste its text.
  3. Add the link of a public job page you want to keep. The server fetches the public page once, reads it, and stores nothing behind a login.
  4. One batch can contain many posters — each role is split into its own job card automatically.
  5. Every card is scored for civil relevance and confidence, checked against jobs already on the site, then parked in the Job Inbox.
  6. You edit anything the machine got wrong and press Approve & publish. Only that button writes to the live jobs list.
Compliance: submit only material you received or own — posters and forwards you were given, public pages, your own scans. This tool does not log into WhatsApp, LinkedIn or any portal, and does not scrape other people's accounts or private groups. AI output is a draft; you verify every field before publishing.
